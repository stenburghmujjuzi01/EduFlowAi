About EduFlow

                  

                    
EduFlow helps you learn, practice and track progress with an AI tutor across chat, WhatsApp and this platform.

                  

                  

                    
What you can do here

                    
Six AI modes (Tutor, Coding Assistant, Study Partner, Quiz Master, Research Assistant, Career Coach), plus notes, a study planner, flashcards, quizzes, assignments, a whiteboard, a code editor, a math workspace, and progress tracking with certificates.

                  

                  

                    
Available everywhere

                    
Use it here on the website, or message EduFlow directly on WhatsApp - sign up with the same phone number to keep progress in sync across both.

                  

                  

                    
Version

Getting started

          

            
How do I start a course?

            
Open AI Chat and tell EduFlow what you'd like to learn, or use the "Start new course" button next to the mode switcher. You can also message EduFlow directly on WhatsApp.

          

          

            
What are modes?

            
Tutor, Coding Assistant, Study Partner, Quiz Master, Research Assistant and Career Coach are different AI personas tuned for different kinds of help. Each keeps its own separate chat history.

          

          

            
Can I use this on WhatsApp too?

            
Yes - your account and progress are shared between WhatsApp and the website, as long as you sign up with the same phone number.

          

        


        

          
Courses & certificates

          

            
How do certificates work?

            
Pass your final assessment (6/10 or higher) to receive a downloadable, verifiable PDF certificate with a unique certificate code.

          

          

            
Can I switch topics mid-course?

            
Yes - use "Start new course" (or the equivalent per mode, like "Start new topic") at any time. Your previous course stays saved and searchable.

          

          

            
How is my skill level decided?

            
EduFlow asks a few quick questions when you start a topic and adjusts as you go based on your answers and quiz results.

          

        


        

          
Account & access

          

            
Guest vs. registered

            
Guests get a limited chat with a daily message cap and nothing saved. Signing up (free) unlocks every AI tool, saves your chat history per mode, and tracks your progress.

          

          

            
I forgot my password

            
From the login screen, use your email to sign back in, or reach out via Contact us below if you're locked out entirely.

          

          

            
Can I delete my account?

            
Yes - go to Settings → Account → Danger zone and request deletion. We'll follow up by email to confirm before anything is removed.

          

        


        

          
Troubleshooting

          

            
The AI isn't responding

            
Check your connection and try again. Guests are limited to a small number of messages per day - signing up removes that limit.

          

          

            
My colors/theme look broken

            
Try switching themes once in Settings → General, or a hard refresh (Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+R). If it persists, let us know via Contact us.

          

          

            
Voice input or read-back isn't working

            
Voice features need microphone permission and a supported browser (Chrome/Edge work best). Check Settings → Voice for your chosen voice and language.

Didn't find what you needed? Send us a message and we'll get back to you.

Overview

          

            
EduFlow is a learning platform accessible on the web and WhatsApp. This page explains, plainly, what we store and why - not a full legal policy, just the practical version.

          

          

            
The short version

            
Guests: nothing is saved. Registered users: we save what you create (chats, notes, tasks, flashcards) so you can pick up where you left off. We don't sell your data or show ads.

          

          

            
Who this applies to

            
Anyone using EduFlow on the website or WhatsApp, whether browsing as a guest or with a registered account.

          

          

            
Questions?

            
Use Help & Support → Contact us, or the "Request data / deletion" option under Your choices.

          

        


        

          
Guests vs. accounts

          

            
Browsing as a guest

            
Nothing you say in guest mode is tied to an account or saved beyond this browser session - no chat history, no progress tracking. Messages are rate-limited per device/IP to prevent abuse, not to identify you. Closing the tab or clearing your browser erases everything from that session.

          

          

            
With an account

            
We save your chats (organized by mode/session), notes, tasks, flashcards and progress so you can pick up where you left off, on the website or WhatsApp. Your account is identified by your email (website) and/or phone number (WhatsApp).

          

          

            
Switching from guest to registered

            
Signing up starts a fresh account - anything said as a guest beforehand isn't retroactively imported, since it was never linked to you in the first place.

          

          

            
Linking WhatsApp and web

            
Signing up on the website with the same phone number you use on WhatsApp links the two, so progress carries over either way.

          

        


        

          
What we collect

          

            
Account info

            
Email, WhatsApp number, display name, and anything you enter (chat messages, notes, tasks, flashcards, custom quizzes, community posts).

          

          

            
Usage & progress

            
Which modes/features you use, quiz and assessment scores, XP/level, lesson progress, and certificates earned - so we can show your stats and improve the product.

          

          

            
Device preferences

            
Theme, language, text size, voice choice and chat drafts are stored locally in your browser (localStorage), not on our servers, and stay on this device only.

          

          

            
What we don't collect

            
We don't sell your data, don't run ads, and guest sessions aren't linked to any identity at all - there's nothing to tie them to you even if we wanted to.

          

        


        

          
How it's used

          

            
Running the product

            
Showing your chat history per mode, tracking progress and streaks, issuing certificates, and powering search across your own notes and chats.

          

          

            
Generating AI replies

            
Chat content is sent to our AI provider to generate replies, scoped strictly to the current mode/session - we don't send your whole account history with every message.

          

          

            
Support & improvement

            
Feedback you submit helps us fix bugs and prioritize features. We may look at aggregate, anonymized usage patterns (e.g. "how many people use Quiz Master") to improve the product.

          

          

            
What we never do

            
Sell your data to advertisers, or use your private chats to train models for other companies.

          

        


        

          
Third-party services

          

            
Profile picture (Gravatar)

            
If you use "Use email profile photo," we look up a photo via Gravatar using a one-way hash of your email - we don't store or share your actual email address with them.

          

          

            
Authentication & database (Supabase)

            
Accounts, passwords (hashed, never stored in plain text), and all your saved content are managed via Supabase.

          

          

            
AI provider

            
Chat messages are sent to our AI provider solely to generate a reply - scoped to the relevant mode/session, not your whole account.

          

          

            
WhatsApp (Meta)

            
If you message us on WhatsApp, that conversation is also subject to WhatsApp/Meta's own privacy terms in addition to ours.
